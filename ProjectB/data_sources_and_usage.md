# Data Sources and Usage

NYC DOT publishes [DOT Traffic Speeds](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Transportation/DOT-Traffic-Speeds/i4gi-tjb9) through NYC Open Data. The dataset contains aggregated speed estimates for road segments. Original download shards remain byte-identical. See `data/source_snapshot.json` and `data/geometry_manifest.json` for queries, download and snapshot dates, SHA-256 hashes, and the road-geometry version. The portable package stores the shards under new paths. Derived arrays follow the registered graph construction, cleaning, labels, and causal feature rules. See the [method and data contract](reference/method_and_data_contract.md). Rights in the original and derived data do not transfer to this project.

On 2026-11-12, the project reviewed the [NYC Open Data FAQ](https://www.nyc.gov/opendata/get-started/FAQs) and [Public Policies](https://cityofnewyork.github.io/opendatatsm/publicpolicies.html) for use, attribution, and disclosure of modifications. See also the [NYC Open Data Terms of Use](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/stories/s/Terms-of-Use/k9k7-3cje). The package identifies the source, snapshot, and derived modifications. It does not assert a CC0, CC-BY, or ODbL license.

The source may change, be corrected, or contain gaps. The city offers no guarantee of completeness, accuracy, or fitness for a particular purpose. This project constructs its own derived labels, graph, and models. The results represent course research and carry no official congestion determination or city endorsement. The original portable package was assembled for local coursework reproduction. It contains no private GPS trajectories, credentials, or Conda binaries.
