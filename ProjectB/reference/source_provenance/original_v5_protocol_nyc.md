## Data Source, Calendar, and Road Network

- Use the official NYC DOT Traffic Speeds API `i4gi-tjb9` and its public use terms. Times use America/New_York civil time. Date selection consults only public metadata, speed-free coverage counts, and timestamps. Metadata checks found coverage in September 2026 and the first week of October. - The half-open periods are 07:00–09:00 and 17:00–19:00. API shards are split by week and AM/PM, using `data_as_of`, `link_id`, `speed`, `travel_time`, and `status`. Lock the full protocol first. For holdout CSVs, transfer checks are limited to headers, row counts, and hashes. Parse holdout speeds or labels only after freezing models, rules, data, weights, thresholds, and the sample-support contract.
- Use only geometry metadata from 2026-06-21 07:00 to 08:00. For each segment, fix the most frequently occurring directed polyline of the same name, with lexicographic tie-breaking. Prefer the official `encoded_poly_line`. If it cannot be fully decoded, verify the coordinate text. Remove geometry without an explicit positive length or with out-of-bounds coordinates. Sort by `link_id` to assign stable integer keys starting at 1. Connect tail endpoints to head endpoints when distance is ≤25 m, or ≤80 m within the same road corridor, with local tangent angles below 100 degrees. Exclude nearby reverse loops. Extract one-hop graph neighborhoods with at most 12 nodes and center position 0. Record isolated nodes, edges, provenance, and geometry gaps. The segment list is frozen before training.
- Within each five-minute half-bin, deduplicate by segment and distinct `data_as_of` values and take the median for records with the same timestamp. Conservatively admit reports with status 0, positive `travel_time`, and speed in (0,85] mph. Each half-bin requires at least one qualifying estimate. Average the two half-bin medians with equal weight to obtain the ten-minute estimate. Missing either half-bin, no collection, or insufficient evidence is treated as missing. Risk and labels follow the Chicago definitions. The historical baseline uses at least three days for the same directed segment, ten-minute local time bin, and weekday or weekend type. Each training sample uses only strictly earlier dates. Development, calibration, and holdout freeze their respective training periods. Two consecutive valid estimates, each strictly below 40% of its corresponding historical mean, form a positive. With sufficient label evidence, a current speed returning to its threshold or above is a recovery negative. Unknown labels remain in the coverage audit. Added features do not change eligibility or labels.
- The forecast decision occurs when the target bin starts and the most recent bin ends. Only source timestamps earlier than the target bin are eligible, and lags do not cross segment, peak-period, or day boundaries. `data_as_of` is an offline availability proxy because the source has no independent arrival timestamp. New York probe sources, five-minute half-bins, geometry-based connections, roads, and years differ from Chicago. They do not establish the coursework's continuous five-minute criterion.

## Models, Pairing, Thresholds, and Acceptance

- B reruns the original V4 P1 + Mean recipe, reusing R2's 40 features, graph model, conditional BCE, weight 0.35 for the ranking term on the hardest quarter of recovery negatives, and routing. New York lacks the Chicago bus and GPS message counts. The corresponding six fields and their missingness indicators remain structurally missing. Road length and direction come from the fixed geometry. Refit preprocessing and weights. B and V5 share the same graph, sampled support, random draws, and training step count.
- X1 adds causal within-bin recovery features. It uses the ratios of five-minute half-bin speeds in the previous two bins to the applicable ten-minute historical baselines, differences, and valid-report support. It also uses changes from an earlier to the most recent half-bin and whether the previous bin's last half-bin remains below the threshold. All half-bins precede the target decision.
- X2 adds to B an auxiliary regression for the current bin's log speed ratio with a shared backbone. It trains only on the known-risk group, with SmoothL1 beta 0.2 and weight 0.30. X3 combines both changes. The main label, current-bin ground truth, and future records are excluded from features and eligibility. Outside supervised sampling, they are excluded from inference inputs.
- First run B, X1, X2, and X3 with Mean, seed 11, and all three forward folds. Rank candidates by macro mean AP rounded to six decimal places. Break ties by lower FP/day at 60% recall, then higher development F1, then registration order. Confirm the shortlisted candidates with three pooling methods and seeds 11/22/33 on each fold, matched to B by seed. Apply the same rule to select pooling. The update budget follows V4, with 12 epochs, batch size 128, Adam at 0.001, width 32, four heads, two layers, the same fixed sampling seed, and a budget of 12,000 negatives. Select each fold's epoch by risk AP. Fix the final checkpoint for each recipe and pooling method to the median best development epoch while running the full budget. Select and freeze the threshold by maximum positive-class F1 on the complete 28-day calibration period, with V4 tie-breaking.
- Evaluate on all graph windows with known labels. Low-risk, recovery, and positive windows all receive scores. AP uses `average_precision_score`.
